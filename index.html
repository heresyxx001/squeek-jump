How it works

  • Jump & slide: Space / JUMP hops rocks, crabs and driftwood. Down arrow / S / SLIDE ducks you under low seagulls — jumping into a gull wipes you out. Rocks speed up as they reach you, and crabs sidestep while they scuttle.
  • Shells are a gamble: shells and starfish float in the jump arc above obstacles. Grab them with a late, risky jump for points — missing one costs nothing.
  • Combo: clear obstacles back-to-back to build your multiplier up to ×5. Barely clearing one is a perfect — bonus points. Only a wipeout (or letting the combo timer run out) drops it.
  • The live wheel sets the rules: Nap (wheel stopped) = slow, sparse obstacles and shells worth double. Jog = normal. Zoomies (wheel flying) = faster everything, golden shells worth big points, and a shorter combo timer.
  • Bank wheel passes: every obstacle you clear banks 1 pass. At 10 banked, spend them on one thing: a Shield that eats one hit, or a Double Jump for one extra mid-air leap.
  • The sunset moves: every 100 m the sun sinks, the sky deepens and the tide rolls in — wet sand slows you briefly, dry sand is faster.
